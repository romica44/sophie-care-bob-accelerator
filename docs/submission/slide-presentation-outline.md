## Slide 1 — Title

**SophieCare Bob Accelerator**

Turn a complex healthcare AI idea into demo-ready software faster with IBM Bob.

## Slide 2 — Problem

Developers lose time understanding unfamiliar repositories, writing documentation, planning tests, and preparing software for delivery.

In specialized domains like healthcare, onboarding is even slower because teams need both technical and domain context.

## Slide 3 — Solution

SophieCare Bob Accelerator shows how IBM Bob acts as an AI development partner that understands the full repository context and helps developers move faster.

The project uses SophieCare AI Surgical Co-Pilot as a realistic healthcare AI codebase.

## Slide 4 — How IBM Bob Is Used

IBM Bob supports:

- Repository understanding
- Architecture explanation
- Documentation generation
- Test planning
- Refactoring suggestions
- Submission readiness review

## Slide 5 — Demo App

The demo app is a Next.js prototype showing a surgical intelligence dashboard for ophthalmology training.

It includes simulated phase analysis, safety-aware feedback, resident training insights, agent traces, and responsible AI disclaimers.

## Slide 6 — Repository Evidence

The GitHub repository includes:

- IBM Bob Evidence Center
- Bob prompt playbook
- Bob task plan
- Session log template
- Export folder for the official IBM Bob report
- Submission-ready documentation

## Slide 7 — Architecture

Frontend: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS

Backend: Next.js API route with typed validation

AI Workflow: Bob-assisted development process + simulated healthcare AI agent workflow

Deployment: Vercel

## Slide 8 — Business Value

Bob can reduce repetitive engineering effort for teams working with complex repositories.

The workflow applies to consultancies, healthcare software teams, enterprise product teams, and training environments.

## Slide 9 — Market Opportunity

Software teams spend significant time on onboarding, documentation, testing, and codebase understanding.

A Bob-powered workflow can help teams improve delivery speed, quality, and confidence.

## Slide 11 — Responsible AI

The healthcare prototype is educational only.

It is not a medical device and cannot be used for diagnosis, treatment, or real intraoperative decisions without clinical validation and regulatory review.

SophieCare Bob Accelerator proves how IBM Bob helps builders turn ideas into impact faster by understanding code, reducing repetitive work, and improving delivery confidence.
